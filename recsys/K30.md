## The specific hypothesis

Javi already tests uniform cart-weight increases in `javi/known-session` (`c2`, `c4`). K30 instead boosts pending carts in proportion to the shopper's phrase affinity. This can promote a relevant pending cart over a generic item while leaving a neutral-affinity cart alone. It is not a proposal to force every cart into the slate.

Only returning users on `product`/`category` pages with recent views qualify. For each candidate the extra **ranking logit** is:

```text
pending_cart * clip((phrase_affinity - 0.10) / (0.35 - 0.10), 0, 1)
```

Pending means the historical/live cart sets already computed by LF, excluding bought products. Maximum bonus is 1.0, fixed before reading replay labels. The existing affine feature already provides the capped/centered affinity. No extra queries, state, features or inference models are added. The bonus is a ranking heuristic, not a newly calibrated purchase probability.

The three K30 coefficient artifacts are exact copies of their r1/r2/r3 base `known`, `new` and `lq0`, plus `score_patch: "k30"` and provenance. The r2 clone is `lfm_coef_k30.json`; r1/r3 clones are `lfm_coef_k30r1.json` / `lfm_coef_k30r3.json`. Only the r2 pair was used for the chronological replay below; r1/r3 copies are checked for exact coefficients and off-target parity, not credited with that replay result. `LiveFit.set_arms()` recognizes that optional artifact field; all ordinary arms keep their legacy `(w,b)` representation and scoring. An unknown patch rejects the arm update. New-user/home scoring is identical to the matched control. Existing stock, bought/stale-item and valid-ten checks remain in effect. No `live.py`, `serving.py`, training interface or dependencies changed.
